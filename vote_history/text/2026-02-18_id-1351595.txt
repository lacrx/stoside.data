The following items are ordinances for adoption by the City 
Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA. Ordinances are laws of the City of Oceanside and 
require introduction and adoption at two separate City Council meetings

one meeting as an emergency measure). The City Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA has

adopt the ordinances below in a single vote. There will be no discussion of the
