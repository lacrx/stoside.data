GENERAL COUNCILMEMBER COMMENTS – Comments made 
ADOPTION OF ORDINANCES 
The following items are ordinances for adoption by the City Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA. Ordinances 
are laws of the City of Oceanside and require introduction and adoption at two separate City Council 
meetings (urgency ordinances are an exception, and may be introduced and adopted at one 
meeting as an emergency measure). The City Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA has adopted a policy that it 
is sufficient to read the title of ordinances at the time of introduction and adoption, and that full 
reading of ordinances may be waived. After the City Attorney has read the titles, the City 
Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA may adopt the ordinances below in a single vote. There will be no 
discussion of the items unless requested by members of the City Council/HDB/CDC/OPFA or the 
public through submittal of a Request to Speak form prior to the commencement of this agenda 
item.
