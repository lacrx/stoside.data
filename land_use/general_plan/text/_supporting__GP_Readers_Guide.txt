Oceanside General Plan Update  
December 2025 Reader’s Guide  
The Oceanside General Plan (GP), Smart and Sustainable Corridors Specific Plan (SSCSP), Climate 
Action Plan (CAP) Update, and supporting Environmental Impact Report (EIR) were initially pre-
sented for public review in June 2024. Following public review, the documents have been revised 
in response to input received during the public review period. This Reader’s Guide outlines the 
major changes made to the GP Elements in response to community and stakeholder comments. 
Changes are presented by element, but the following changes apply throughout the document: 
• 
Minor adjustments to the SSCSP boundary (see SSCSP Reader’s Guide); 
• 
All references to clustered housing in South Morro Hills removed; and 
• 
Identifying El Corazon as a designated Park.

EFFICIENT AND COMPATIBLE LAND USE ELEMENT (ECLU) 
The ECLU was updated for accuracy on items that have changed since the 2024 public review draft, 
and timelines for Action Items were updated. The North County Multiple Habitat Conservation 
Program (2003) and the Loma Alta Creek Watershed Management Plan (2003) were added as re-
lated plans. The GPLU map was updated (Figure 3-3). The maximum FARs for Professional Com-
mercial (CP) and General Industrial (GI) were updated to 1.25 and 1.5 respectively. The buildout 
estimates were updated to reflect a slight decrease in residential units and a slight increase in non-
residential square footage. Additional policies and text were added to address safety and compati-
bility with the Camp Pendleton Marine Corps Airport and the Oceanside Municipal Airport.

VITAL AND SUSTAINABLE RESOURCES ELEMENT (VSR) 
The VSR was updated for accuracy on items that have changed since the 2024 public review draft, 
and timelines for Action Items were updated. The element was updated to better reflect the priori-
ties and policies of the Draft Sub Area Plan and the City’s plans for future conservation mapping 
efforts. Of all the elements, the VSR saw the most significant updates from the June 2024 public 
review draft with the most extensive changes found within section 5.2 Open Space, Vegetation and 
Wildlife.
