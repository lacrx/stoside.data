SYNOPSIS 
Staff recommends that the City Council introduce an Ordinance to amend Chapter 5 of 
the Oceanside City Code to align the City Code with changes in laws, regulations, and 
best practices concerning the operation of regulated mobility devices (including e-bikes) 
within the City limits. 
BACKGROUND 
In recent years, Oceanside has witnessed an influx of electric bicycles ("e-bikes"), electric 
scooters, motorize skateboards, etc., traveling on City streets. Along with traffic violations, 
traffic collisions involving bicycles, electric bicycles, electric personal assistive mobility 
devices, electrically motorized boards, low-speed vehicles, motorized scooters, shared 
mobility devices, and other similar vehicles is a growing concern. The Police Department 
reported 188 traffic collisions involving bicycles and/or e-bikes from Jan. 1, 2020, through 
July 1, 2023. Of these 188 collisions, 67 involved e-bikes, while 121 involved traditional 
bicycles. In 61 percent of these collisions, the bike and/or e-bike operator was determined 
as the party at fault for that collision. On the average, 16 percent of all bicycle/e-bike 
collisions resulted in serious injury. Twenty-one percent involved juveniles and, in the 
majority of cases, 60 percent, the individual(s) on bicycles/e-bikes were not wearing 
helmets. 
In response to bicycle and e-bike safety concerns, the Police Department has increased 
its enforcement and education efforts this past year. Among its education efforts, the 
Police Department has created an educational flyer that was distributed to the Oceanside 
Unified School District, which was then disseminated to the parents via their education 
portal. This flyer addressed proper safety guidelines when operating bicycles on the 
road. The Police Department has also created educational pages specific to the operation 
of e-bikes and rules of the road on its webpage. This information provides details on what 
qualifies as an e-bike, e-bike rider requirements, where e-bikes can be ridden, e-bike 
types, and the rules of the road. These web pages are located on the Oceanside Police 
Department website and are accessible to the public. In addition to the education flyers

ANALYSIS 
California Vehicle Code Section 21282 allows cities to enact ordinances regulating the 
time, place, and manner of the operation of electric personal assistive mobility devices as 
defined in California Vehicle Code Section 313. Similarly, California Vehicle Code 
Section 21225 allows cities to enact ordinances to regulate the registration of motorized 
scooters and the parking and operation of motorized scooters on pedestrian or bicycle 
facilities and city streets and highways, if that regulation is not in conflict with the California 
Vehicle Code. 
The proposed ordinance would promote the safety of persons using bicycles, e-bikes, 
electric personal assistive mobility devices, electrically motorized boards, low-speed 
vehicles, motorized scooters, shared mobility devices and other similar vehicles, defined 
in the ordinance as "regulated mobility devices." Specifically, the Ordinance would

Staff believes that the proposed local ordinance would be an important tool to increase 
safety throughout the City. This ordinance would offer a locally controlled, education- 
based enforcement mechanism as an alternative to monetary court-issued fines. The 
Police Department believes this method would be a more effective deterrent with youth 
who are not always financially responsible for their California Vehicle Code violation fines. 
This ordinance would authorize a peace officer or employee charged with enforcing the 
City's traffic laws and regulations to enforce the ordinance. In lieu of filing charges in court 
for a violation of the ordinance, which would likely result in the imposition of a fine in the 
amount of $50, the Police Chief or designee may allow a violator of this chapter to 
complete a police department-provided Bicycle/E-bike Safety Educational Program for 
regulated mobility devices. The Police Department designed Bicycle/E-bike Safety 
Educational Program would be regularly offered to the participants free of charge. The 
ultimate goal is to educate bicycle, e-bike and other regulated mobility device operators 
on safety and road rules.
